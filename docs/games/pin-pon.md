# Pin Pon

**Goal:** Score five points before the AI opponent.

**Mechanics and controls:** Move the mouse vertically to control the player's paddle. The ball bounces off the top and bottom edges; a paddle hit sends it back with increasing horizontal speed. If the ball passes a paddle, the other side scores and the ball restarts.

**Difficulty:** Choose Fácil, Medio, Difícil, or Imposible. The setting changes how quickly the AI paddle follows the ball.

**Completion and scoring:** The first side to five points ends the match. A rematch resets the score. Scores are session-only and are not saved to player records.
