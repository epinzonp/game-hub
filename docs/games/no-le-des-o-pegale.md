# No Le Des o Pégale

**Goal:** Hit as many characters as possible before the 30-second timer ends.

**Mechanics and controls:** Choose a difficulty, start the round, then click a hole when a character appears. Only a hit on the currently active hole adds a point. Characters appear at random positions on a nine-hole board and disappear after a short window.

**Difficulty:** Fácil: 1,000 ms between appearances and 800 ms visible; Medio: 700/600 ms; Difícil: 500/400 ms; Imposible: 300/250 ms.

**Completion and scoring:** The timer reaching zero ends the round and displays the score. Retry resets the timer and score. Scores are not saved to player records.
