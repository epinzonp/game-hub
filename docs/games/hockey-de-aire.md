# Hockey de Aire

**Goal:** Score against the opponent by sending the puck through their goal.

**Mechanics and controls:** Choose AI or the option labeled Jugador. Move the lower paddle by mouse or touch on the table. The puck bounces off the side walls and paddles. A puck crossing the goal opening scores for the opposite side; shots outside the opening bounce back. The current implementation does not wire separate input for the upper paddle when AI is off.

**Difficulty:** AI matches offer Fácil, Medio, Difícil, and Imposible. Higher settings make the AI paddle move faster toward the puck.

**Completion and scoring:** The first goal wins the match. A win against the AI awards 100 coins to the signed-in player and shows a green animated `100+` reward indicator at the end. Defeats and matches against another player award nothing. The match score and result remain in the current session only.
