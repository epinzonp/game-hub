# Tiragol

**Goal:** Score as many goals as possible by aiming around the goalkeeper.

**Mechanics and controls:** Drag the ball to choose a shot direction, then shoot. Each shot is a goal, a save, or off target. The keeper randomly covers left, center, or right; shots aimed in that direction are saved. A shot outside the target area misses. After seeing the result, start the next attempt.

**Completion and scoring:** The current run ends automatically after 10 goals and requests a return to the hub. The visible goal count is the run's score. Each goal awards 10 coins to the signed-in player's balance and shows a green animated `10+` reward indicator; misses and saves award nothing. Since the run has points while playing, leaving after it starts — including after the tenth goal — shows the existing confirmation dialog.
